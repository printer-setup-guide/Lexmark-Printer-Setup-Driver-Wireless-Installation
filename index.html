Lexmark Setup Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find Lexmark printer IP address?

Touch the Network icon on the main screen or print a Network Setup Page from Settings > Reports > Network Setup Page.

How do I clear 31.xx Replace Unsupported Cartridge error?

Ensure you are using genuine Lexmark Unison toner cartridges intended for your specific regional printer model.

How do I reset Lexmark wireless settings?

Go to Settings > Network/Ports > Wireless > Restore Factory Defaults.
